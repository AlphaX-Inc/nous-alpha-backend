**10 Anti-Patterns:**
1. Do not treat apparent stability as genuine stability (private credit low volatility = infrequent pricing, not low risk)
2. Do not confuse the testing phase with the all-clear (Stage CD → DE: conviction deepens but imbalance grows larger)
3. Do not assume the Fed can always save the day (Greenspan Put is constrained in fiscal dominance)
4. Do not invest based on need (reaching for yield to meet a return target is the most dangerous form of risk-taking)
5. Do not mistake the dollar's current strength for permanent reserve currency status (Triffin Dilemma is structural)
6. Do not treat collective stabilization mechanisms as solutions (London Gold Pool, Greenspan Put, private credit marks — all suppress price discovery and defer adjustment)
7. Do not use duration as a safe haven in a fiscal dominance regime (traditional "long bonds in recession" trade may be catastrophically wrong)
8. Do not confuse "everyone owns it" with validation (saturation is a sell signal, not a buy signal)
9. Do not ignore the insurance company / reinsurance amplifier (PE → insurance → offshore reinsurance = CDO-squared of this cycle)
10. Do not wait for the moment of truth to reduce risk (reduce risk at Stage EF, not Stage GH)
